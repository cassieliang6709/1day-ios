# 1Day logo v2

## Assets

- `1day-logo-lockup-v2.png` — primary transparent lockup for light surfaces.
- `1day-app-icon-v2.png` — 1024 × 1024 App Store icon artwork on mist blue.
- `1day-logo-monochrome-v2.png` — transparent single-ink lockup for tinting and watermark use.
- `1day-logo-mist-preview-v2.png` — presentation preview on the mist-blue brand surface.

## Core colors

- Brand blue: `#1677FF`
- Deep navy: `#102343`
- Mist blue: `#EAF3FF`
- Warm white: `#FAF8F2`

## Usage

- Keep the mascot visually dominant; the `1day` signature is secondary.
- Use the primary lockup on light or mist-blue surfaces.
- Use the monochrome PNG as a template/tintable asset for one-color applications and small video watermarks.
- Do not add an extra icon container around the lockup.
- Preserve clear space equal to at least one eye diameter around the mark.

These v2 assets are used by the landing page and the iOS App Icon asset catalog.
