## 描述

### zh-Hans

```text
每天拍几段 2 秒的片子，和朋友放在同一个房间里。

拍照
• 点一下拍 2 秒。
• DV 机风格的取景框：REC、日期和边框直接拍进片子。
• 0.5x、1x、2x 三档变焦，也能横着拍。
• 前后摄同时拍：你的脸放在小窗里，可以拖动、缩放。

和朋友一起
• 建一个房间，把 6 位邀请码发给朋友。
• 大家拍的片子按日期排好，点哪天看哪天。
• 点朋友的头像，只看 TA 的片子。双击头像，拍一拍 TA。
• 双击片子回爱心，长按选别的表情。房间里也能聊天。
• 朋友拍了、拍了拍你、给你留言时，你会收到通知。

自己的日记
• 「我的」把你拍的片子按天排好，每天可以记一个心情。
• 点右上角的日历，选一天，只看那天的片子。

做成 vlog
• 选几段，一键拼成一条视频，在 iPhone 上生成。
• 配原声、音乐，或两者一起，也能自己录旁白。
• 「帮我挑」：AI 从一天里挑几段，再起一个标题。
• 存进相册，或直接分享。

账号和隐私
• 用 Apple 或 Google 账号登录。换手机后，房间还在。
• 只有你发到房间的片子会上传，房间里的人都能看到。存在「我的」里的片子只在你的 iPhone 上。
• 「帮我挑」只发送片子的时间和字幕文字，不发送视频。

支持中文和英文。
隐私政策：https://1day.liangyue.site/zh/privacy
联系：liangyue3666@gmail.com
```

### en-US（ar-SA 同）

```text
Film a few 2-second clips a day and keep them in one room with your friends.

FILM
• Tap once to film 2 seconds.
• A camcorder-style frame: REC, the date and the border are filmed into the clip.
• Zoom at 0.5x, 1x or 2x, and film in landscape.
• Film front and back at once. Your face sits in a small window you can drag and resize.

WITH FRIENDS
• Make a room and send your friends its 6-character code.
• Everyone's clips are sorted by date. Tap a day to see it.
• Tap a friend's avatar to see only their clips. Double-tap it to nudge them.
• Double-tap a clip to send a heart, or long-press for other reactions. Rooms have a chat too.
• Get a notification when a friend films, nudges you or leaves a comment.

YOUR OWN DIARY
• Mine sorts your clips by day. Add a mood for each day.
• Tap the calendar in the top corner and pick a day to see only that day.

MAKE A VLOG
• Pick a few clips and stitch them into one video, made on your iPhone.
• Use the original sound, music or both, or record a voiceover.
• "Pick for me": AI picks a few clips from your day and gives the video a title.
• Save it to Photos or share it.

ACCOUNT AND PRIVACY
• Sign in with Apple or Google. Your rooms come back on a new phone.
• Only clips you send to a room are uploaded, and everyone in that room can see them. Clips in Mine stay on your iPhone.
• "Pick for me" sends only the times and caption text of your clips, never the video.

Available in English and Simplified Chinese.
Privacy: https://1day.liangyue.site/privacy
Support: liangyue3666@gmail.com
```
